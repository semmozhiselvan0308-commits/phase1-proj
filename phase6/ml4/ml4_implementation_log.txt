Interpretation:
- HIGH means observed activity is materially above comparable historical hours.
- LOW means observed activity is materially below comparable historical hours.
- The anomaly score describes observed deviation; ML3 predicts a future proxy label; NP3 applies operational rules.
- Disagreement between the three mechanisms is evidence to investigate, not a software defect.
